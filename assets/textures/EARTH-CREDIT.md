# Earth texture

NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble: Next Generation, January 2004 base map.

Source page: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-map/

Source image: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-base/january/world.200401.3x5400x2700.jpg

Local derivatives: earth-4k.webp (4096 x 2048, quality 86) and earth-2k.webp (2048 x 1024, quality 84), resized from the 5400 x 2700 JPEG using ImageMagick. Cloud-free monthly composite, not live imagery. Atmosphere and lighting are simulated. No NASA endorsement implied.

Europe detail: the matching 21600x10800 NASA JPEG (`world.200401.3x21600x10800.jpg` at the same source directory), cropped to longitude -20 to 40 degrees and latitude 30 to 65 degrees. Desktop crop 3600x2100; phone crop 2048x1195. Rebuild with crop_europe.py. The active scene uses the 2K global texture plus the regional crop, not the 4K global texture.
